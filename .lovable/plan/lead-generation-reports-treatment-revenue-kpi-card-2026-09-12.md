# Lead Generation Reports — Treatment Revenue KPI Card

Add a display-only KPI card next to **Treatments Converted** that surfaces the revenue already generated by those conversions.

## What changes

- Insert a new KPI card labeled **"Treatment Revenue"** immediately after the **"Treatments Converted"** card in the KPI row.
- Value: `totals.revenue` (sum of `amount_paid` from bills linked to matched leads), formatted as ₹.
- Subtitle: "Revenue from converted treatments" plus "Avg ₹X per conversion" when `totals.treatments > 0`.
- Icon: reuse `IndianRupee` or `TrendingUp` from existing imports.

## What does NOT change

- No new queries, tables, migrations, or calculations.
- No changes to filters, pagination, campaign table, or attribution logic.
- `totals.revenue` is already computed; this plan only exposes it in the KPI row.

## Files

- `src/pages/reports/LeadGenerationReports.tsx` — add one entry to the `kpis` array and keep the existing 4-column responsive grid.
